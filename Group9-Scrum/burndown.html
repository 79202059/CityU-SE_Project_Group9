How to keep this page current

1. After the weekly standing meeting, append or edit the matching row in data/burndown-sprint-N.csv (remaining story points and a one-line event).

2. Flip forecast from 1 to 0 when a day becomes history, and move the today flag to the current working day.

3. Regenerate everything:

python tools/make_burndown.py — rewrites charts/*.svg and this file.
